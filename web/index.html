A SPENDING LIMIT FOR AI AGENTS

Give your AI agent a budget, not your wallet.

Leash puts an AI agent’s money behind hard limits that are enforced on the blockchain. Anyone, including your partners, can check those limits in under a minute. No wallet, no account.

SAMPLE · like a company card for an AI
Per payment limit0.25 NEAR
Per period limit0.5 NEAR
Approved payees2 accounts
Emergency freezea guardian can pause
Who is in chargethe NFT holder

The four questions people ask.

Can the agent run off with everything?

Not past its limits. Each payment and each period has a hard cap written into the contract.

Who is in charge?

Whoever holds the NFT. Transfer the NFT and control moves with it.

What if something looks wrong?

A guardian can freeze the agent. Loosening limits has to wait out a delay first.

Can I check this myself?

Yes. Paste the address. The check runs in your browser against a public data source.

Where teams could use it.

These are ideas, not customer claims.

A treasury assistant

An agent that pays routine invoices without ever holding the whole treasury.

Vendor payments

Pay only approved accounts, up to a set amount each time.

Trading with a loss cap

Let an agent trade while the worst case stays a known number.

Partner pilots

Hand a partner a one-page check instead of a technical walkthrough.

Gas and Allowance Reservation Bounds

          

            In NEAR protocol, FunctionCall access keys with allowances reserve the full attached gas fee upfront from the allowance.
            Unlike on-chain execution where unused gas is refunded to the account balance, access key allowances are never refunded.
          

          
1Attached Gas: 30 Tgas

          
2Upfront Key Reservation: ~0.0289 NEAR per call

          
3Key Allowance: 0.25 NEAR -> ~8 invocations capacity

          
4Key Allowance: 1.00 NEAR -> ~34 invocations capacity

          
5Window Cap: Enforced per sliding period minus one bucket
